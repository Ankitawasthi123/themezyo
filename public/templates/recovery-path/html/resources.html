Recovery FAQ

How do I know which level of care is right?

Our admissions team uses a confidential assessment to understand your needs and recommend an appropriate starting point.

Will insurance cover treatment?

Many plans cover substance use treatment. Our benefits team can verify coverage and explain it clearly.

Can my family be involved?

Yes. Family education and counseling can be an important part of sustainable recovery.
